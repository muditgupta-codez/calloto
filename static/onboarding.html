Step 2: Set up call forwarding

Forward unanswered calls to your Calloto number. Takes 2 minutes.

iPhone

  1. Settings → Phone → Call Forwarding
  2. Forward when unanswered → enter your Calloto number
  3. Done

Android

  1. Phone app → Menu → Settings → Call forwarding
  2. Forward when unanswered → enter your Calloto number
  3. Done
