FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You keep your existing business number. Calloto gives you a UK number to forward unanswered calls to. Your customers never see our number — the text-back comes from us using your business name.

How does call forwarding work?

It's a built-in feature on every UK mobile. On iPhone: Settings → Phone → Call Forwarding. On Android: Phone app → Settings → Call forwarding. Or dial **61*your-calloto-number#. We walk you through it during onboarding — takes 2 minutes.

What if the caller has a withheld number?

We can't text withheld/private numbers. These show in your dashboard as "not texted." Typically less than 5% of calls.

What if the caller isn't on WhatsApp?

We try WhatsApp first (85% of UK adults use it). If they're not registered, we automatically send SMS instead. You don't do anything.

Can I customise the text-back message?

Yes. Set your business name, price range, and booking link during onboarding. We provide a pre-filled template you can edit anytime from your dashboard.

How much does call forwarding cost?

A few pence per forwarded call (standard network rate). Most tradespeople forward 10-30 calls/month — that's under £1 to recover hundreds in bookings.
