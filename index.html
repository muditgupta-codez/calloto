Questions

Do my customers need an app?

No. They just get a normal text (or WhatsApp message) — it works on any phone, no install.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Pingback attaches to your existing number. You keep taking calls the same way — we just catch the misses.

What if I'm not on a job and just don't want to answer?

You can set your own hours and quiet time. Outside them, calls go straight to auto-text-back.

What does the text say?

Your name, "thanks for calling", a rough price range you set (e.g. "boiler repairs from £120"), and your booking link. You can edit the message anytime.

Is this just for plumbers?

Any trade: electricians, builders, roofers, locksmiths, gardeners, cleaners — anyone who loses jobs to missed calls. Early access prioritises plumbers & electricians.
